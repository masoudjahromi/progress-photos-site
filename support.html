Progress Photos: support

Questions or problems? Email masoud.j.dev@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don’t send body photos.

The camera doesn’t work

Open the iPhone Settings app, find Progress Photos and turn on Camera. You can also add photos from your library.

A photo won’t import

If the photo is stored in iCloud, connect to the internet and try again.

Moving to a new phone

The app keeps no copy of your photos anywhere else. Back up your phone, then restore that backup on the new one.

Deleting your data

Delete photos or poses inside the app, or delete the app. Copies in device backups, and anything you saved or shared, stay until you delete them there.
