Progress Photos: privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version: The app does not upload your photos. It sends us nothing about you. There is no account, no server and no tracking.

What we collect

The app collects nothing. It has no sign-up, no login and no analytics. It does not contain advertising or any third-party tracking code, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your photos

Photos you take or import are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Photos you take in the app are kept only in the app. Imported photos are copies. Deleting a photo in the app never deletes anything from your Photos library. We cannot see your photos and have no way to access them. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Automatic alignment

To line photos up, the app finds the position of your shoulders and hips. This runs entirely on your phone using Apple's built-in tools. The result is a zoom and position setting saved next to the photo. It is not used to identify you, measure you or judge your body, and it is never sent anywhere.

Permissions

Camera: only to take a photo when you ask. Add to Photos: only when you choose to save a comparison. Importing uses Apple's photo picker, so the app sees only the photos you select.

Sharing and backups

A comparison leaves the app only when you tap Share and choose where to send it. If you have turned on iPhone backup with Apple, your device backup may include this app's photos. That backup is yours, is controlled in your iPhone settings, and is not visible to us.

Deleting your data

Delete any photo or pose inside the app at any time. Your photos stay in the app until you delete them or the app. Deleting the app removes what it stored on your phone. It does not remove copies in your device backups, or comparisons and time-lapses you saved or shared. Delete those where you keep them. If removing a photo's file fails, or the app closes partway through, a hidden copy can remain inside the app, and in backups of your device. The next time it opens, the app sets that copy aside, and it deletes it when it opens more than 30 days after that. While no photos are saved in the app, it keeps such copies until you delete the app. The app sends us no copy, so we have none to delete.

If you email us

We then receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you, which is in our legitimate interest, and delete it within 12 months of our last reply. Our email provider processes it for us and may do so outside the European Union. You can ask to see, correct or delete it, or object to our using it. You can also complain to your data protection authority, which in the Netherlands is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
